# War Era v0.27: what changed and what it breaks here

Source: the [v0.27 release notes](https://app.warera.io/article/6ade08f7899288ce9f0bfe36) (published 2026-10-25, last edited 2026-10-26). API and config facts below were checked against the live API (`api2.warera.io`) and the game's Next.js bundle on 2026-10-26. Re-probe before relying on anything marked *unverified*.

## The short version

The player job market is gone. Companies now run on NPC **employees**, and the only way to work for another player is as a **manager**. Entrepreneurship, worker fidelity, the self-work tax, country specialisation, deposits and party ethics are all removed. The production-bonus model this repo reverse-engineered no longer matches the game.

## Game changes that matter to our tools

### Employees replace workers

- Each company gets 3 candidates a day at 00:00 UTC. Hiring costs 10 energy. Rerolling the set costs 10 energy, doubling each time.
- The new **Break Room** upgrade caps headcount: level 1 = 1 employee (free), level 2 = 2 (20 steel), level 3 = 3 (80 steel). Config: `gameConfig.upgradesConfig.breakRoom.levels[n].stats.maxWorkers`. A company's level is in `company.activeUpgradeLevels.breakRoom`.
- Stats: Production, Luck and Responsibility, each rolled 1 to 4. Training Potential is 3 to 10. Starting age is 18 to 45, and employees age 1 year per day. Retirement starts at 65.
- Output is `Production ÷ 4` per hour, boosted by Management, happiness (±1% per point away from 50), the tool, time spent on the same good (+1% per day, max +20%) and a core-region company (+5%).
- **Wages are set by the employee and paid hourly and automatically.** Wage = one hour of base output (`Production ÷ 4`) × the money value of 1 production point (from raw-material trades over the last 7 days) × the employee's own expectation (70% to 130%). Greedy +20%, Modest −15%. If the owner can't pay the full hour, nobody works that hour. Wages leave the game as a money sink.
- Self-work in your own companies still exists and costs 10 energy.
- Tools: 6 types. Bonuses run from +3% (Common) to +65% (Mythic). Tools last about 500 worked hours and can't be traded.

### Managers

- One manager job per company. The owner sets pay per Management point, and applying hires the manager instantly. There is a 24h lock after hiring.
- Each Management point gives +2% employee production, up to +22%. This stacks with the owner's own Management, up to +44% combined.
- Pay is collected hourly, and the country takes a **manager tax**. Pay stays within 80% to 120% of the average (`managerOffer.getWageStats`).

### Skills

- **Entrepreneurship is removed** and its points are refunded. Everyone gets 1 free reset.
- Energy: `100 + 10/level` (was 30 base). Production (self-work): `30 + 3/level` (was 10 base).
- Management no longer caps hires. It gives +2% employee production per point.
- **Leadership** is new and unlocks at level 10. It improves training cards and event odds.
- `gameConfig.skills` keys now: energy, stamina, health, hunger, attack, companies, production, criticalChance, criticalDamages, armor, precision, dodge, lootChance, management, leadership.

### Country economy

- **Country specialisation is removed. Deposits are off** (`gameConfig.region.depositsEnabled: false`). `gameConfig.company.depositResourceBonus` (30) is still in config but dead.
- Strategic resources no longer boost production or development. Each one pays its holder a daily income equal to the development of the country it originally belonged to.
- Taxes are now `country.taxes = {income, market, manager}`. **The self-work tax is gone.** Income tax is charged on employee wages. Each 1% of income tax costs employees 1 happiness.
- Conquered regions send the original owner 10% of the income tax (`gameConfig.country.conqueredRegionCoreTaxPercent`).
- `country.income` is a new breakdown: `{development, strategicResourcesBonus, employeeIncomeTax, managerIncomeTax, govType, total}`.
- Everyone gets 1 free company move.

### Government types replace party ethics

- `country.govType` is one of `republic`, `nationalistState`, `homelandFront`, `federation`, `plutocracy`, `autocracy`, `mercenaryState` (enum from the bundle). It's `provisional` by default. Ireland had no `govType` field on 2026-10-26, which presumably means provisional.
- Changing the type has a 30-day cooldown. Some types forbid alliances, pacts or a sworn enemy, and those are dropped immediately.
- Parties lose their ethics and can no longer start resistance.

### Other changes our tools may touch

- Wooden cases: Common 20-40, Uncommon 40-70, Rare 80-130, Epic 160-260 production points. Odds are now 60 / 25 / 12 / 3%.
- Daily reward day 7: 35 → 50 money. The new missions are train an employee, resolve an event and care for an employee. "Find a job" now means taking a manager job. The move-company mission is gone.
- Unlock levels changed: market and military units at 5, crafting and the manager market at 10, parties at 15. Running in elections now needs level 20.
- Map: 726 → 742 regions, plus a new country (Maldives). Several regions were renamed: renamed ones keep their IDs, owner and companies. Capitals moved in Cyprus, Ghana, Sudan and the USA. The 71 removed links close 10 days after the update.

## API changes

| Endpoint | Status |
|---|---|
| `worker.getWorkers` | **Removed** (404) |
| `workOffer.getWorkOffersPaginated` | **Removed** (404) |
| `employee.getCompanyEmployees`, `employee.getById`, `employee.getCompanyRoster` | Exist, but return 403 "API tokens cannot access this endpoint" with a key: browser session only |
| `employeeEvent.*`, `manager.getMyJob`, `tool.getMine`, all "my" endpoints | Browser session only |
| `work.getStatsByCompany {companyId}`, `work.getStatsByUserId {userId}` | Key required. 31 daily rows of `{dailyDate,total,automatedEngine,selfWork,wage,workerProd,employeeProd}`. The best public view of employee output (company level, daily) |
| `manager.getByCompanyId`, `managerOffer.getByCompanyId`, `managerOffer.getWageStats`, `company.getProductionBonus`, `upgrade.getUpgradeByTypeAndEntity` | Public |
| `poll.*`, `userSkillPreset.*`, `article.saveArticle` | New, not used here |

Per-employee stats, traits, happiness and wages can't be read with an API key. Tools should be calculators driven by user input plus public prices and config, not per-employee monitors. Transactions have no employee or manager pay type, only the existing `wage`.
